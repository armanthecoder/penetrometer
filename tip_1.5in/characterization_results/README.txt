Double-head (0.25 in tip + 1.5 in compression head) characterization results.

Samples
- Red wet: 4 samples
- Red semi-wet: 3 samples

Separating the tip and compression head stages
The stages are split automatically from the force-time graph. When the compression head
touches the soil, the contact area increases and the loading rate (dF/dt) jumps. The split
is the start of the first loading ramp at least 2 times faster than all earlier ramps.
Before it the tip carries the load alone; after it the compression head is also in contact.

If no such jump is found, the split is the start of the last continuous ramp to the peak.
The split is checked on each sample's plot. Manual exceptions: red wet sample 4 and
red semi-wet sample 1.
